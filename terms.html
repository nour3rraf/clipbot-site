Terms of Service

What ClipBot is

ClipBot is a private, single-operator tool. It edits short clips from livestream recordings and uploads them to accounts owned by its operator. It is not offered to the public and has no user sign-ups.

Who can use it

Only the operator of the ViralClipsToday accounts. Connecting an account through TikTok, Google or Meta sign-in grants ClipBot permission to upload videos to that account only.

Content

Clips feature publicly streamed content and credit the original creator in the caption. Rights holders who want a clip removed can ask through the ViralClipsToday channel, and it will be taken down.

Platform rules

ClipBot follows the terms and community guidelines of TikTok, YouTube and Instagram. It does not post the same video more than once per account.

No warranty

ClipBot is provided as-is, for the operator's own use.
